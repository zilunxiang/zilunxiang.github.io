Zilun (Zizivich) Xiang

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Minnesota, advised by Prof. Peter Bruggeman and Prof. Aditya Bhan. My research focuses on non-equilibrium plasma transport, reaction kinetics, and physics-based multiphysics modeling.

Research

My current work combines experiments and modeling to study transport and chemical reactions near plasma-driven interfaces. I use COMSOL, MATLAB, Python, and high-performance computing to build and test models against diagnostic measurements.

Selected project descriptions and research updates will be added as this site develops.
