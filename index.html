I am a Ph.D. researcher in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Minnesota, advised by Prof. Peter Bruggeman and Prof. Aditya Bhan.

I received my B.E. in Theoretical and Applied Mechanics from the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), where I was advised by Prof. Yu Liu and Prof. Kefu Huang.

Research

My research focuses on plasma-liquid interfaces. I primarily use laser-induced fluorescence (LIF) to investigate how reactive species are transported from a plasma into a liquid and how interfacial processes shape that transport.

Alongside these experiments, I develop physics-based models of coupled transport and reaction processes. On the modeling side, I am increasingly interested in AI for plasma science (AI4Plasma), especially approaches that combine data-driven learning with physical constraints to interpret measurements and improve predictive models.

Current projects

Plasma–liquid transport diagnostics

Using laser-induced fluorescence and complementary optical diagnostics to study reactive-species transport across plasma–liquid interfaces.

PFAS reaction–transport modeling

Developing coupled transport and reaction models for plasma-driven PFAS decomposition, with attention to interfacial processes, kinetics, and mass balance.

Physics-aware AI for PDEs

Exploring neural operators and other physics-aware learning methods for time-dependent PDEs, with the longer-term goal of improving plasma and reacting-flow models.
